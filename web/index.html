About this project

Aspirants study from many notes and PDFs, and general chatbots can invent answers. This assistant uses Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG): it first retrieves the most relevant passages from your own material, then builds the answer from those passages and cites them. If nothing relevant is found, it says so.

Notes→Chunks→BM25 index→Top passages→Answer + sources

How it works

Chunking. Notes are split into passages of about 80 words with 20 words of overlap.

Retrieval. BM25 ranks passages by query terms, rare terms count more, and exam and subject filters narrow the search. Unicode tokenising supports Hindi.

Answering. The best sentences from the top passages are returned in order, each marked with its source number.

Quiz. Sentences become fill-in-the-blank MCQs. Wrong options come from other facts in your notes.

Plan. Days are split into learning, revision and mock-test phases, with weak subjects given double weight.

Limits

This version runs fully in the browser with no server or AI model, so answers are extracted sentences and not rewritten explanations. It needs plain-text notes. Next steps: semantic embeddings, an LLM for fluent answers, PDF parsing and previous-year paper analysis.
